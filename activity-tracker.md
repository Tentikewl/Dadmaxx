## Developmental activity ideas (age 8 months)

### Indoors
- Mirror play — self-recognition emerging at this age
- Peek-a-boo and hiding games — object permanence development
- Stacking / knocking blocks — cause and effect
- Simple board books — language development
- Music with instruments (shakers, drums) — auditory and motor

### Outdoors
- Park swings — vestibular stimulation, big hit at this age
- Grass time / barefoot sensory play
- Beach or water play (supervised)
- Pram walks with narration — language input
- Playgrounds with slide (supported)

### Social
- Catch-ups with other babies — parallel play stage
- Playgroup sessions — structured social exposure
